About

Aftertone is a music discovery tool that recommends songs based on how they actually sound rather than by who else has listened to them. When finding a song, users can tailor their recommendations based on what they’re looking for—for instance, if they’re looking for a song with similar instrumentals, the same vocal style, or same emotional meaning within the lyrics. Aftertone adjusts it’s algorithm in order to match your needs, providing the recommendation that fits just right.

This project is currently in beta testing. Please come back another time for music recommendations :)
