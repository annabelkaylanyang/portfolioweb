Hi, I’m Annabel!

I love design because to me it serves as the sweet intersection where I can both express my creativity as an artist and wire my thinking into how to create something suited for others’ needs. I’m most interested in storytelling, whether that be through interaction, motion, or something visual that evokes emotion, places, and connection in others.

In my free time, you’ll probably find me cooking! I enjoy experimenting with making dishes from different cuisines and love to collect cool plates/kitchenware around the world. My favorite season is winter (I absolutely love the snow) and on most days I’d choose coffee > tea, though it’s a close tie. I don’t get homesick often, but if I do it’s probably because I miss my dog and my plants (I have too many to bring across the country </3).
